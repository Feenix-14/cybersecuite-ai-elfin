Your everyday defence

Small habits. Strong protection.

01

Add a second layer

Enable two-factor authentication. Prefer an authenticator app or passkey when available.

02

Keep software updated

Install updates for your phone, browser, and apps to fix known security weaknesses.

03

Pause before you click

Check the actual website address. Open the official app directly when a message feels suspicious.

04

Keep backups

Back up important files regularly and keep a separate copy protected from accidental changes.
